Discussion Topic: Discussion on cooperation between iQIYI and VCCorp in Vietnam

Executive Summary

The meeting focused on discussing legally compliant operations and the long-term development orientation of iQIYI in the Vietnamese market.

VCCorp introduced its support capabilities in Public Relations (PR), Government Relations (GR), and content compliance. Both sides focused on discussing content moderation standards, crisis management, and identifying cooperation options and upcoming execution timelines.

PART I: CURRENT STATUS AND CORE NEEDS OF iQIYI IN VIETNAM

At the beginning of the meeting, iQIYI representatives introduced their business operations in the Vietnamese market, while clarifying urgent challenges and needs related to compliance with legal regulations.

1. Market Positioning and Legal Compliance Foundation

Objective of Long-term and Legal Operations

iQIYI aims for long-term development and legal operation in the Vietnamese market.

According to information shared during the meeting, iQIYI was granted a certificate of eligibility for content self-classification by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism of Vietnam (MOC) in 2023. Currently, iQIYI is discussing specific cooperation directions with domestic telecom operators, banks, and several state-owned enterprises.

Legal Compliance as the Highest Priority

iQIYI identifies compliance with Vietnamese legal regulations as a prerequisite and the most important factor in operation. The business has partnered with a law firm in Vietnam and conducted various legal research and preparation activities from earlier stages.

2. Current Core Challenges

Unclear Boundaries Regarding Allowed Content Levels

Recently, certain content on the iQIYI platform was requested for removal by the Vietnam Cinema Department. The incident highlighted differences between content classification standards in Vietnam and other markets, particularly regarding sensitive content, strong expression content, or content intended for audiences aged 18 and older. iQIYI needs to define clearer boundaries for specific content permitted for dissemination in the Vietnamese market.

Crisis Management and Negative Public Opinion Handling

In the context of continuously updated legal regulations, iQIYI recognizes potential risks if the business fails to adapt promptly to new requirements from regulatory authorities. When these issues attract attention from authorities or relevant organizations, iQIYI desires external partner support to minimize negative impacts on brand image.

Need for GR and PR Support

  • iQIYI currently has a dedicated internal team focused on user acquisition and content marketing activities.
  • Therefore, the current priority need does not lie in executing additional standard marketing activities.
  • Instead, iQIYI seeks long-term support from VCCorp in the following areas:
    • Building and strengthening brand image in the Vietnamese market.
    • Media crisis management.
    • Government Relations (GR) and regulatory affairs.

PART II: VCCORP'S CORE COMPETENCIES AND INITIAL COOPERATION DIRECTIONS

Based on the needs shared by iQIYI, VCCorp presented its ecosystem advantages and specific support directions that can be implemented in Vietnam.

1. Ecosystem and Local Market Experience

Comprehensive Service Ecosystem

VCCorp has built an ecosystem comprising media, content marketing, technology, data, and entertainment. This ecosystem is capable of fulfilling the complete operational chain, from market research and creative planning to media execution and performance optimization.

Experience Partnering with Authorities and Organizations

VCCorp has participated in numerous joint programs and projects with government agencies, public organizations, and industry associations. Through these, VCCorp has accumulated extensive experience in strategic communications and public engagement, particularly in cultural activities, social impact, and youth engagement.

2. Proposals on Content Compliance and Crisis Management

Establishing Content Review and Classification Mechanisms

VCCorp proposed that iQIYI proactively review its content library and categorize it into two groups:

  • White Zone: Standard content evaluated as suitable for distribution in the Vietnamese market.
  • Grey Zone: Content containing sensitive elements, violence, or aspects requiring further inspection/verification before distribution.
  • For Grey Zone content, iQIYI should proactively conduct internal reviews and implement removal or distribution restriction measures when necessary.

Connecting Advisory Resources from the Vietnam Cinema Department

For Grey Zone or sensitive content, VCCorp can assist iQIYI in connecting with appropriate officers and personnel at the Vietnam Cinema Department for professional consultation. The goal is to clarify whether specific content is permitted for dissemination in the Vietnamese market.

Setting Up an Online Public Sentiment Monitoring System

VCCorp recommended that iQIYI deploy an internet and social media monitoring system (Social Listening). This system aims to detect early discussions, reactions, or negative sentiment related to iQIYI's content in Vietnam, enabling proactive development of suitable handling strategies.

Noting Risks of Adverse Sentiment Driven by Competitors

VCCorp advised iQIYI to monitor risks where competitors might exploit sensitive elements in the platform's exclusive content to trigger negative public opinion. Such discussions could attract scrutiny from the public or authorities, thereby increasing brand risk.

3. PR and GR Support

Crisis Management Requires Multi-Party Coordination

VCCorp transparently shared that within the complex environment of regulatory relations in Vietnam, 100% elimination or resolution of all crises cannot be guaranteed. However, VCCorp commits to leveraging appropriate resources to support connection, consultation, and coordinated resolution. At the same time, VCCorp recommends that iQIYI maintain a proactive, cooperative attitude and rapid response during crises or regulatory matters.

Implementing Brand Affinity Enhancement Activities

For the current situation, VCCorp suggested researching and developing Brand Love initiatives in the next phase. Through positive media activities, iQIYI can gradually enhance public perception, preference, and affinity toward the brand in Vietnam.

PART III. NEXT STEPS FOR COOPERATION

At the conclusion of the meeting, both parties agreed on tentative timelines and coordination plans for the next steps.

1. Plan for Connecting Cinema Department Resources

Initiating Connections This Week

Right after the meeting, VCCorp will begin identifying suitable contacts at the Vietnam Cinema Department to support iQIYI's content compliance consultation needs. If favorable, contact introductions will be made at the earliest possible time. According to the meeting discussions, contact information is expected to be provided within this week or the following week.

2. Providing Initial Cooperation Options

Sending Initial Action Plan This Week

VCCorp commits to developing and delivering an Initial Action Plan to iQIYI within this week.

Proposing Multiple Cooperation Options

As iQIYI is in its budget planning phase for the upcoming year, VCCorp was requested to present multiple options for consideration.

These options should differentiate across:

Budget: Estimated investment levels.

Scope of Work: Corresponding deliverables and task items.

Cooperation Model: Implementation and collaboration frameworks between both parties.

The objective is to provide iQIYI with sufficient basis for evaluation and selection aligned with business priorities and budget plans.
